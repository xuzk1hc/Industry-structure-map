# Style Profiles

Use one profile as the starting direction for the HTML report. The renderer may reinterpret the profile while preserving its visual character and hard restrictions.

## Chip-Tech Blue

Best for semiconductors, HBM, optical modules, AI infrastructure, data centers, and electronic equipment.

- Character: precise, technical, restrained, high-density
- Palette: deep blue, ice blue, cyan, limited orange/red emphasis
- Domain signals: circuit paths, wafer layers, package stacks, data flow
- Graph behavior: luminous but readable lines, strong lane hierarchy, compact company badges
- Avoid: excessive glow, generic cyberpunk decoration, neon text everywhere

## Consulting Clean

Best for cross-industry strategy, diversified industrial chains, and audiences that prioritize readability.

- Character: analytical, calm, editorial, executive-friendly
- Palette: white or light grey base, dark ink text, limited blue/green/orange accents
- Domain signals: disciplined grids, clear annotations, restrained data highlights
- Graph behavior: simple lines, strong whitespace, clear grouping
- Avoid: decorative dashboards, oversized cards, marketing-landing-page composition

## Industrial Engineering

Best for manufacturing, equipment, batteries, rockets, robotics, chemicals, and heavy industry.

- Character: structural, mechanical, process-led, robust
- Palette: charcoal, steel grey, safety orange, signal yellow, technical blue
- Domain signals: process stages, assembly layers, engineering labels, material transformations
- Graph behavior: strong physical-flow arrows, visible merge points, clear equipment/process distinction
- Avoid: playful illustrations, soft lifestyle visuals, overly abstract relationships

## Editorial Infographic

Best for public-facing explainers and reports that should feel like a polished long-form industry poster.

- Character: approachable, narrative, visually varied, still analytical
- Palette: context-specific multi-color system with restrained background
- Domain signals: small illustrations or icons tied to real products and processes
- Graph behavior: guided reading path, strong section rhythm, selective callouts
- Avoid: sacrificing relationship accuracy for decorative composition

## Custom Profile

When the user requests another style:

- translate the request into visual character, hierarchy, palette, typography, graph language, creative freedom, and hard restrictions
- name the profile
- avoid combining more than two profile families
- explain which profile rules are inherited and which are overridden
